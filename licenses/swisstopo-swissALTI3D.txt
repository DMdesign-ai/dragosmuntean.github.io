Hero scene data: public/hero/

Terrain (base-height.webp, detail-height.webp) and baked lighting
(base-light.webp, detail-light.webp) are derived from swissALTI3D
(Federal Office of Topography swisstopo), 2024 and 2025 survey tiles,
2 m grid, LV95 2638-2648 E / 1153-1163 N. Base resampled to ~20 m,
detail (2640.5-2645.0 E / 1157.8-1161.8 N) to 4 m.

Route (trail.json, trail-mask.png) is the "Eigertrail" path from
swissTLM3D (swisstopo), Eigergletscher to Alpiglen, 5.9 km.
