## What it is

An Airbnb-shaped booking experience for Corippo, a village in Ticino with a population you can count. The premise is that a place that small does not need a platform, it needs a page.

## Why it is in Labs

It was an experiment in how far AI assistance could carry a whole designed site rather than a component or a page section: layout, copy, imagery treatment, booking flow.

It is not a live product and was never trying to be. It got far enough to answer the question I had, and then it went on the shelf.
